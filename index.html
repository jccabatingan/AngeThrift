How ordering works

From bag to doorstep

No app to download, no account to make. Just browse, add to your bag, and send us your order.

1

Browse the Shop

Check the Shop page for pieces currently available, or scroll our live Facebook feed above for the newest drops.

2

Add to Bag

Tap "Add to Bag" on anything you love. Your bag stays saved on this device even if you close the tab.

3

Send Your Order

At checkout, send your order straight to our Facebook Page or email — whichever's easiest for you.

4

We Confirm & Ship

We'll reply to confirm availability, payment, and shipping — then your good find is on its way nationwide.
